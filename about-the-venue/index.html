About the Venue

Queen Sirikit National Convention Center (QSNCC)

How to get there

By MRT

Take the MRT to Queen Sirikit National Convention Center Station (Exit 3), QSNCC is located right in front of the railway station.

By BTS

Take the BTS Skytrain to Asok interchange station, then transfer to the MRT Queen Sirikit National Convention Center Station (Exit 3).

By Bus

The public bus lines 136, 185 and 501 stop right in front of QSNCC. The main entrance is within a short walk from the bus stop.
