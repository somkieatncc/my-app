Thanyaphat


Headline : Relieve Muscle Pain Medicated Cream
Brand of Product : Thanyaphat Brand
Product's Type/Category : Healing and Soothing Cream
Detail of Product/Service : Thanyaphat features a smooth, lightweight cream-lotion texture that spreads easily, absorb quickly, and leaves no sticky residue. Its carefully selected ingredients include Menthol, Eucalyptus Oil, Wintergreen Oil, and Moisturizing Agents, providing a cooling and soothing sensation while helping maintain skin moisture. It is suitable for relieving discomfort associated with tired or overworked muscles, including the neck, shoulders, back, arms, legs, and knees. Its cooling effect also provides a relaxing sensation after physical activity, massage, or long working hours.

Company name : Thanyaphat 41 Co., Ltd.
Company Profile : Thanyaphat is a Thai herbal wellness brand specializing in topical products designed to provide a refreshing and soothing experience for everyday muscle discomfort. Our flagship product is THANYAPHAT Cooling Relief Cream, which combines traditional herbal ingredients with a convenient, easy-to-use cream formulation. With years of experience in the Thai market, Thanyaphat serves individual consumers, retailers, pharmacies, massage and wellness businesses, and distribution partners. We are committed to consistent product quality, accessible pricing, and building long-term partnerships with our distributors.

As we expand internationally, Thanyaphat is seeking importers, distributors, wholesalers, and business partners to introduce Thai herbal wellness products to new markets worldwide.
